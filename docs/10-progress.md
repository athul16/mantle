**Look and feel.** Every screen follows the Mantle prototype (`docs/design/prototype.html`, see `13-design-system.md`). Prototype features the backend doesn't support yet are listed below under "Prototype features not built yet".

**Company side** (`/app/<slug>`, opens on Jobs): jobs (create job dialog), job page with the candidates list, invite page (`/jobs/<id>/invite`: paste or CSV), resend and revoke, assessments library and configure screen, candidates results table, candidate report (answers vs expected, rubric review, flags with snapshots, notes and vote, timeline), team. Plus sign up, log in, onboarding, `/auth/*` callbacks and set password.

**Candidate side** (`/a/<token>`): landing → `verify` (6-digit email code) → `check` (consent, camera, entire-screen share) → `instructions` → `workspace` → `review` → `done`. Server routes under `/api/candidate/*`: `code`, `verify`, `start`, `state`, `answer`, `advance`, `event`, `submit`, and `proctoring/{consent,signals,upload,media}`. An assessment can run as timed sections: each section has its own minutes, "Finish section" / "Next screen" go through `/api/candidate/advance`, steps outside the current section are locked, and some sections show one screen at a time. Extra time stretches sections, screens and rapid items; the overall timer never pauses (D4).

**Engine.** Seven step types: `numeric`, `inbox_reply` (rubric; human review, AI shadow scoring only), `spreadsheet`, `journal_entry`, `document_review` (with an optional "nothing wrong here" confirm, `confirmClean`), `rapid_round` (items one at a time with a server-timed countdown and response times), `multiple_choice` (single exact, or multi with wrong picks costing, floored at 0). Timed sections and one-at-a-time screens (`06-assessment-engine.md`, "Timed sections and rapid rounds"). `/dev/engine` previews any scenario.

**Company report.** Answers next to the expected answer (rapid round items with prompt, values, expected values and response time), rubric review, a Timing card (answers within 2 s of returning to the window, a large rapid round vs other tasks gap; evidence only, the label doesn't change), flags with snapshots, filmstrips and audio, notes and vote, timeline (PRs #39, #45).

**Proctoring (v2, 2026-11-15).** Settings per assessment: camera, screen, require full screen, microphone, gaze check; per-invite waivers for each of them except gaze (which follows the camera). Rules (`src/lib/proctoring/rules.ts`): `left_window`, `paste`, `copy`, `copy_then_leave`, `fullscreen`, `screen_share`, `camera`, `multiple_displays`, `downtime`, `gaze`, `no_face`, `multiple_faces`, `speech`. Camera and screen snapshots every 45 s and on leaving the window; while away, screenshots at +2 s, +6 s, every 10 s and on return; audio clips only around speech; gaze runs on the device and sends events only. The label is severity based: suspicious for combinations (left window with camera or screen trouble, copy then leave, multiple faces, speech with looking away or leaving, or 3+ kinds of flag), review for any other flag, clear for none, with the reasons shown on the report. Nothing auto-rejects. Details: `02-security-and-integrity.md`, `decisions/2026-11-15-proctoring-v2.md`.

## The MVP, piece by piece

1. **Foundation** (done): database, tenant isolation, server-only answer keys.
2. **Company side** (done): companies, teams, URLs, assessments, jobs, invites, results table and candidate report with rubric review and notes.
3. **Candidate side** (done): invite link → 6-digit email code → consent → system check → instructions → timed workspace (inbox left, task right) → review → submit. Server-owned timer, autosave, resume after refresh.
4. **Engine** (done for MVP step types): turns a scenario into tasks with each candidate's own numbers, then scores them. `numeric`, `inbox_reply` (rubric; AI scorer in shadow only), `spreadsheet`, `journal_entry`, `document_review`, `rapid_round` and `multiple_choice` are built, plus timed sections and one-at-a-time screens.
5. **Integrity** (built, v2): browser signals, copy counter, webcam and screen snapshots, away capture, optional full screen, microphone and on-device gaze, flags with evidence that a recruiter confirms or clears, severity-based label with reasons. Nothing auto-rejects.
6. **Content** (drafts in code): `harbor-lane-bank-rec`, `corporate-ap-ridgeway` and `audit-calder-bluestem` (Examples 4 to 6 in `07-accounting-simulations.md`). Published as v1 for testing (2026-11-17); they still need a browser playthrough and the team's content self-review, and fixes ship as v2. Tax, advisory and forensic simulations are handed off to a separate collaborator.
