# 09 · Go-to-market: accounting

Initial market for the accounting simulations: 10 Triangle employers (6 CPA firms, 4 staffing agencies). Research as of Nov 2026, from firm sites, job postings, and candidate interview reports; single reports are signals, not policy.

## Why accounting firms will listen

- **Talent shortage, not applicant overload.** Accounting degrees fell to 55,152 in 2023-24 (down 6.6% in a year) while 75% of hiring firms planned to hire as many or more grads in 2025 ([AICPA via Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/accounting-grad-numbers-continue-to-decline-despite-hiring-push)). Pitch **proof of skill**, not screening volume.
- **Nobody tests the real work before the offer.** CPA firms screen on personality and fit; skills show up during busy season.
- **Agencies test, but with multiple choice**, which AI handles easily.

**Where we slot in:** CPA firms, between application and fit interviews. Agencies, as a replacement for multiple-choice tests, with a score report they can show clients.

## Existing accounting test vendors

EmployTest (40 MC questions), Adaface, Mercer Mettl (69 questions in 60 min), Kenexa Prove It (Robert Half), Predictive Index (personality, Cherry Bekaert). All are multiple choice or personality; none are job simulations.

## Pitch

- **CPA firms:** "Know before the offer whether an intern can actually reconcile an account."
- **Agencies:** "Swap multiple-choice trivia for a hands-on test you can show clients."
- **Career switchers / non-accounting majors:** the result is proof of ability, which widens the pool firms can hire from.
